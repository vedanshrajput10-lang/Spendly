About Spendly

Spendly is a simple, no-frills way to track daily expenses — organized into whatever sections make sense for you, not a fixed list someone else decided on. Add an expense, tag it with a section, and see where your money actually goes each month.

Features

  • Custom sections with your own colors
  • Monthly view with a spending breakdown
  • Search across all your past expenses
  • Export your data as a CSV file anytime
  • Installable on your phone's home screen
